What the 18 runs reveal

Under the new from-scratch bootstrap conditions (every bot starts identical: bronze pickaxe+axe, level-30 melee, and *nothing* else — no coins, runes, ore, or tools), cooperation stopped being a nice-to-have and became the load-bearing mechanic. A single bot cannot both bootstrap a resource economy (mine/thieve/kill for coins → buy tools/runes → process) *and* specialize hard enough to score. So the interesting signal is which models actually stood up a gather/earn → buy → funnel → specialist chain and sustained it for the hour, versus which flailed on role election, pathfinding, or the lossy ground-drop transfer channel.

The verdict is sharp and model-consistent across events. Gemini 3.5 Flash, GPT-5.5, and Claude Sonnet 5 reliably built genuine supply chains in all three events and posted the top scores (Gemini smith mithril_med_helm 780gp with the smith at Mining 35; Sonnet crafting lvl21/34,732xp; GPT-5.5 smith steel_platebody 2000gp / crafting lvl9). Opus 4.8 built real chains but bled a large fraction of supplier throughput to method-thrash and despawn (crafting: 14 min lost to wool→gold→leather thrash + toll bug, still reached lvl17/25,336xp). GLM-5.2 was the clear laggard: leadership thrashed in every event (three simultaneous self-appointed smiths; a never-resolved b-vs-c crafter fight; a magic run where the caster never secured air runes and finished at Magic level 1). GPT-5.4-mini was middling-to-poor — it worked in smith/crafting-partial but collapsed to three-solo-grinders in crafting (topShare 0.475, all three lvl1) and late role-thrash in magic.

The throughline: the differentiator was not in-game mechanical skill (everyone hit the same pathfinding walls, toll gates, and despawns) but social/organizational discipline — locking one specialist fast and keeping two teammates in a disciplined earn-buy-haul loop for a full hour. Teams that locked roles in <2 minutes and never re-litigated leadership (Gemini, GPT-5.5, Sonnet) converted; teams that let two bots both claim the scoring role (GLM, GPT-5.4-mini) burned the hour refereeing instead of building an economy.

Communication strategies

Communication strategy by model

The single strongest predictor of a working economy was speed and stability of role-lock, since public in-game chat is the only channel and every wasted minute of leadership debate is a minute not spent bootstrapping.

Locked fast, zero thrash (the winners):

  • Gemini 3.5 Flash — smith: agentb volunteered at [01:10] ("I can be the primary smith if you guys want! You two can gather ores"), accepted by [01:30], "No leadership thrash for the whole hour." Magic: caster locked by [00:50]. This consistency is why Gemini scored top or near-top in every event.
  • GPT-5.5 — smith locked in ~2 min ("A will be main smith" [02:05], both suppliers accept immediately); crafting locked by 2.5 min with explicit confirmations ("C confirms agenta crafter", "B supports agenta crafter"); magic had one brief flicker (agenta counter-proposed at [00:55], conceded within a minute).
  • Claude Sonnet 5 — all three events locked in the first 1-2 minutes with explicit acks; crafting locked *instantly* ("i (agentc) will be the designated crafter" [00:25], acked by [04:25]/[05:55]).

Thrashed hard (the failures):

  • GLM-5.2 — smith: all three bots independently declared themselves "designated smith" (agentb 01:20, agenta 03:25, agentc 03:40), needing an explicit tie-break ("Resolve: 1 smith only or we split xp and lose. Deterministic: agenta=smith" [04:20]), then a *second* takeover attempt at 09:50 — "cost roughly the first 10 minutes." Crafting: b-vs-c crafter conflict ran from min 1 to min 39 ("Conflict! Only 1 account xp counts").
  • GPT-5.4-mini — magic: agenta AND agentc both claimed caster ([01:05]/[01:45]), "never explicitly resolved," then a *second* collapse after ~41:00 when agentb re-declared itself caster and "the team fragmented into three bots swapping 4-rune scraps." Crafting: both agenta and agentc self-elected crafter, never resolved (commStyle flagged "chaotic").

Drop-handoff protocol was universal and mostly competent among the winners: a fixed rendezvous tile with coordinate-tagged pings — smith at Falador furnace (2975,3368), magic at Aubury (~3253,3401), crafting at the Al Kharid tanner. The best teams ran explicit handshakes to beat despawn: GPT-5.5 smith "A confirm pickup" + "picked back up to prevent despawn"; Sonnet crafting "only drop hides when i say ready" [11:35] and "after you drop, ping me and wait" [24:05] after losing two batches. Gemini smith even relocated the drop point mid-run from furnace to *inside the bank* ([49:11] "Drop them inside the bank, i am standing here to pick them up instantly!") to beat the despawn timer — an adaptive protocol fix.

Where chatter wasted turns: status pings degenerated into low-information heartbeat spam in the weaker runs — GPT-5.4-mini magic: agentb "emitted the identical stale status 'B farming gp for a caster' ~20 times"; Gemini crafting: agenta spammed the identical "Agenta active at (3240,3290)... 899" heartbeat dozens of times during a 13-min XP stall. And GPT-5.4-mini crafting degenerated into "a chaotic 20+ minute loop of 'drop wool on tile X / no, clear the doorway / drop on my exact tile'" — coordinate renegotiation replacing actual work.

Issues encountered

Recurring game/task issues

1. Ground-drop despawn — the dominant, cross-event failure mode. Because dropping items on the ground is the *only* inter-bot transfer channel, despawn timers silently destroyed supplier output in nearly every run. Smith: Gemini [46:11] "They despawned earlier!", Opus "Sorry missed drops-they despawn fast" / "25 Iron on ground here will despawn!", Sonnet lost mithril twice ([40:25],[56:30]). Crafting: Opus "Ferrying is too lossy (hides despawn)... 14 despawning", Sonnet "Lost most hides to despawn!" [11:35] and "Lost that batch to despawn - i was mid-craft!" [24:05]. Magic: GLM [51:15] "runes despawned at bar." Worst-hit: Opus 4.8 crafting (bled ~half its supplier throughput) and GLM-5.2 magic (transfers repeatedly lost).

2. Al Kharid toll gate — a hard economic wall, especially for crafting/smith. Suppliers with 0 coins were physically stranded on the wrong side of the gate from the specialist. GLM-5.2 crafting is the extreme case: "suppliers couldn't cross the Al Kharid toll gate to reach the crafter," so the funnel *structurally never fed the scorer* (agenta "im at gate 3267,3227, 0 coins, need 10gp toll"). Opus crafting: toll bug "permanently locked agentb out of Al Kharid... clicking 'yes,ok' always registers 'no'," forcing a full role migration from agentb to agenta. Also hit GPT-5.5 (crafting/smith), GPT-5.4-mini (magic coal/rune access), Gemini crafting ([36:45] "I have 0gp for the al kharid gate").

3. Coal starvation — the universal smith bottleneck. Steel = 1 iron + 2 coal, so coal was the binding resource in every smith run: GLM "coal-sourcing pivots at 35:10/38:55/43:00/50:40," Opus "Stop iron. Need coal only. Coal coal coal!", GPT-5.5 "Need coal urgently" [21:40]. Analogously, air-rune starvation was the binding magic bottleneck — and the teams that never solved it scored near-zero (see GLM magic below).

4. Deaths to scorpions / dark wizards / guards. Deep-mine king scorpions killed suppliers repeatedly in smith (Gemini agentc [44:20], Opus agentc *twice* losing its pickaxe, Sonnet three separate death events). Magic dark wizards killed the caster and cost a pooled 2488gp stash (Gemini magic [19:45]). Worst: Opus/Sonnet smith suppliers each dying twice, and Gemini magic's catastrophic coin loss.

5. Pathfinding / "can't reach" / stuck-in-geometry. GLM magic: agentb "trapped in wizard tower basement (ladder bugged)" for large stretches. GPT-5.4-mini crafting: "Cannot reach Sheep / Stuck at (3209,3214)" for 20+ minutes. Sonnet magic: agentb stuck in Barbarian Village houses [18:15]-[26:55]. GPT-5.5 magic: 15 "can't reach" hits picking up drops the caster was standing on.

6. Method / economics thrash. Opus crafting burned ~14 min researching dead ends (wool → gold amulets "needs mining lvl40, all bots lvl1" → leather) before the real loop started.

Chat / SDK-layer issues — for the SDK maintainer

Chat / SDK-layer problems for the maintainer

1. Hard message-length truncation (~78-80 chars) — universal and load-bearing. Every single one of the 18 runs reports it, and multiple confirm it is a *transport* cap, not a display artifact: Sonnet smith — "reward.json chat[].text fields are identically capped, confirming a transport-level cap not a display artifact"; GPT-5.4-mini smith — "reward.json chat entries confirm the truncation is in the stored text field." Concrete damage: coordinates and quantities — the exact payloads coordination depends on — get clipped off the end. GLM smith: "steel platebody (2000g\*\*\*\*\*ill" and "Mine ore, smelt bars at f" (furnace coord lost). Agents *noticed and complained in-band*: Gemini smith [01:05] "Agentb, your message got cut off. Who should be the smith?"; Sonnet magic [35:50] "please always include coords when dropping" — literally asking teammates to re-send coords truncation had eaten. This is the #1 fix: raise or eliminate the cap, or at minimum guarantee trailing coordinate tokens survive.

2. Ground-drop is the ONLY item-transfer channel, and it is lossy. There is no direct trade/give. Every rune, ore, hide, and coin handoff goes through drop→coord-broadcast→pickup, which fails via (a) despawn before pickup, (b) empty "0-item" transfers (GPT-5.5 crafting "recovered 0 wool"/"moved 13/0 wool"; Gemini crafting "Dropped 0 soft leathers" for a 13-min stretch), and (c) server-side visibility lag where dropper and picker see different ground state (GPT-5.5 smith [28:40] "items still visible to me on shared tile; waiting for server visibility delay"). In GLM crafting this single-channel design *structurally defeated the whole task* — suppliers stranded across the toll gate had no way to deliver. A direct player-to-player trade primitive would eliminate the largest source of wasted work across all three events.

3. gameMessages polling staleness. Cross-bot state is only observable via getState().gameMessages.slice(-N) on a polling loop, so bots act on stale state and re-ask constantly. GLM smith [09:50] "i don't see anyone smithing at the furnace" acting on stale state; Opus smith re-asked "whats ur smithing lvl?" many times (lines 79/84/108/114) "because they could not reliably see teammate state." Sonnet magic: "one poll returned only the bot's own stale message." A push/event channel or a teammate-state query API would cut the redundant re-polling.

4. Masking / garbled-token artifacts. Asterisk masking corrupts payloads beyond truncation: Opus smith "the hammer \*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*", "agentb(b)=\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*"; GPT-5.5 magic rune-buy confirmations render "air 12, mind \*\*\*\*\*\* 0" (the mind count starred out); GPT-5.4-mini crafting "Using ste\*\*ise pathing" (staircase censored, corrupting a navigation instruction). Looks like a word/profanity filter mangling legitimate game vocabulary and numbers.

5. "undefined" coordinate/NPC leaks. GLM crafting log: "Talking to guard undefined" (x2) when resolving the border-guard NPC — an undefined value leaking into a targeting call.

6. MCP transport failures. Recurring MCP error -32001: Request timed out and -32000: Connection closed on execute_code calls (GLM smith 3x incl. a 60s drop-all call; GPT-5.5 crafting 13 timeouts; Opus magic connection-closed), plus agent scripts crashing on connect (Gemini smith, exit code 1, needing restarts). These stall the action/perception loop independent of the chat layer.

Bottom line

Bottom line

  • Organizational discipline, not game skill, decided the round. Every team hit the same despawns, toll gates, coal/rune starvation, and pathfinding walls. The winners (Sonnet 5, Gemini 3.5 Flash, GPT-5.5) all locked one specialist in under 2 minutes and never re-litigated leadership; the losers (GLM-5.2, GPT-5.4-mini) let two bots both claim the scoring role and burned 10-40 minutes refereeing instead of bootstrapping an economy.
  • Cooperation was genuinely load-bearing this round, and the top models delivered it. The cleanest proofs: Gemini's smith hit Smithing 57 at Mining 35, and Opus's smith hit Smithing 49 at Mining 1 — neither could have self-supplied. Sonnet's crafter reached lvl21/34,732xp from a lvl-1 start only because two teammates bootstrapped the entire toolchain and hide supply. topShare=1.0 with two lvl-1 suppliers is the *correct* success shape here, not idleness.
  • The most egregious failures were resource-bootstrap and role-collapse, not mechanics. GLM-5.2 magic's caster finished Magic level 1 with zero air runes — the team never secured the one resource the task required (suppliers dropped the wrong runes; one teammate was trapped in a tower the whole hour). GLM crafting collapsed to a single self-sufficient carry (427xp, both teammates 0xp, funnel "added nothing"). GPT-5.4-mini crafting collapsed to three solo grinders (all lvl1, topShare 0.475).
  • The ground-drop transfer channel is the SDK's biggest tax on cooperation. With no direct trade primitive, despawn, empty "0-item" pickups, and server visibility lag destroyed a large share of supplier output in nearly every run — and in GLM crafting it *structurally defeated the task* (suppliers stranded across the toll gate). A player-to-player trade API is the single highest-leverage fix.
  • The 80-char chat truncation actively degraded coordination — it clipped the trailing coordinates/counts that handoffs depend on, and agents complained about it in-band ("your message got cut off. Who should be the smith?"; "please always include coords when dropping"). Combined with gameMessages polling staleness and asterisk-masking of numbers/words, the comms layer forced redundant re-asks that ate turns. Raising the cap and adding a teammate-state query would recover real signal.
