Observations

  • Gemini 3.5 Flash beat GLM-5.2 in all nine cells, usually by an order of magnitude.
  • Team size helps, but sublinearly. Gemini’s magic score scaled cleanly (1 → 16 → 26); its smith score saturated at a steel platebody for both n=3 and n=6 — six miners banked ~4M combined Mining XP that the single designated smith couldn’t convert into a higher tier within 30 minutes.
  • Coordination overhead can go negative: GLM’s 6-bot smith run scored below its 3-bot run (28 vs 125 gp) — 1,550 chat messages and a designated smith that only reached Smithing 21.
  • Single trials (k=1): treat gaps smaller than ~2× as noise (e.g. gemini crafting n=1 vs n=3).
The six original n=6 launches fast-failed before any agent ran (the team adapter’s exec command exceeded Modal’s 64 KB ARG_MAX with six inlined instructions) and were relaunched with the fix — their dead job dirs are excluded here.
